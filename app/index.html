    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 — PowerPyx's estimate. No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off for the whole run.

    Golden rules before you start

    • Keep manual saves. There is no mission replay and no New Game+. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave — keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. Positive Mental Attitude is the only potentially missable trophy: one meditation at Jason's house exists only in Chapter 1. See the Missables tab.
    • Scan and study as you go. Scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app (200 of 277 base models needed for 100%) and study animals with binoculars (100 of 255). Progress made during the story already counts.
    • Do optional missions when they appear. You need any 100 for the 100% checklist and there are far more than that — they also introduce new features (fences, the Key Cloner, bank vaults).
    • Avoid the Irredeemable profile. Your Criminal Profile is permanent at its worst level and makes police shoot on sight. See Actions Speak Louder.

    Missable trophies

    Built from the official descriptions and PowerPyx's launch guide. Only one trophy can be missed; everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

    Almost missable — watch out, but not trophy-blocking

    • Race Against Yago (Chapter 3, Yago's street-racing strand) is time-sensitive — it carries an hourglass icon. Do it when it appears; the strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks.
    • Optional missions disappear after a while (black icons with pink letters) and some quest givers go temporarily unavailable, but all trophy-related characters return later and there are far more optional missions than the 100 the checklist needs.
    • Some fences disappear permanently, and a few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that later close — neither affects any trophy, because new fences unlock and the magazines also spawn elsewhere.
