Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10

Built from the official trophy descriptions and PowerPyx's roadmap. One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission replay — but free-roam continues after the story, so almost everything can be cleaned up afterwards.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, scan and study as you go 60–80 hours

    Finish the five chapters and take optional missions as they appear — any 100 of them count toward the 100% checklist, and there are far more than 100. Scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with the binoculars whenever you see them: 100% needs 200 of 277 base-model vehicles and 100 of 255 animals. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — it is the only trophy that may be missable. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots, no mission replay).

    Trophies: all 12 story trophies, the final-mission gold, Cal's fishing line, Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone questlines, and the first meditation.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% completion checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion — but it is the long grind: 300 stunts, all shooting ranges (42 gold medals), all drone races, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 Target Decks, 15 of 18 meditations, and any 70 of 80 challenges (you may skip the hardest 10). Most of it overlaps with the trophy list, so work down the checklist and the trophies fall together.

    Trophies: A Long Road (gold), Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, One Last Thing, Maximum Withdrawal, Pest Control, Fair Game.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands that count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete) and sweep up whatever is left: collectible card sets, WAiNK locations, vehicles, money trophies and the odd one-offs. Five trophies are still unconfirmed at launch (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — check the guide notes for each before chasing them.

    Trophies: Strangers & Freaks (silver), Don't Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, Whip It Good, Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self, Little Boxes, On the Spot — then Vice Got a Price!

Missable trophies

Why the rest cannot be missed

  • Story trophies (12): they unlock by playing the five chapters in order. No chapter trophy can be skipped.
  • Character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret): quest givers may go temporarily unavailable during the story, but every one returns and their quests stay available after the story in free-roam.
  • Strangers & Freaks, robberies, collectibles and activities: all available after the story. Optional missions that disappear do not affect any trophy, and there are far more than the 100 optional missions the 100% checklist needs.
  • Save insurance: keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves) and rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a chapter-start save is your only rewind — and never use cheats on a trophy save: cheats disable trophies and saving.
